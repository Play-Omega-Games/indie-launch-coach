# Store page review

Read the public page (or the text the owner pastes). Never log in to Steamworks. Report in three groups: **Must fix** (platform rules), **Should fix** (guidelines), **Nice to have**.

## Must fix (Steam rules)
- Capsules show a readable title or logo.
- At least 5 screenshots, gameplay only: no concept art, awards or marketing text.
- Short description is plain text, a few hundred characters, with nothing time-sensitive.
- The page only shows features and content that will be in the launch build.
- No links to other websites in the description.
- At least 5 tags before launch.

## Should fix (guidelines)
- The first screenshot and first video show the core loop within seconds. The first video on the page also becomes the short looping preview Steam shows in lists.
- The short description answers: what do I do, and why will I enjoy it?
- "About this game" uses short headings and a few GIFs or screenshots.
- Supported languages and system requirements match the build.
- The date display (exact, month, quarter, year, Coming Soon) matches what you know.

## Output
1. The three lists.
2. Two rewrites of the short description and one of the opening of "About this game", in the owner's voice.
3. Buttons: "Approve", "Edit", "Redo", "Not now". The owner pastes approved text into Steamworks.
