# Trailer and screenshot checklist

## Screenshots
- Steam rule: at least 5, gameplay only, 1920x1080 minimum, 16:9.
- Steam rule: mark at least four as suitable for all ages if they are, so they can show in more places.
- Guideline: one screenshot per idea (core loop, variety, a big moment, progression, a quiet beauty shot).

## Trailer
- Guideline: real gameplay in the opening seconds; the hook early; the game's name and home base at the end.
- Steam builds the short looping preview from the first video on your page, so put your strongest gameplay video first.
- Guideline: readable with the sound off; no claims you cannot show.
- Next Fest: a public trailer on your page by the registration deadline is needed to be considered for the official festival trailer.

## Shot list prompt
List 8 to 12 shots the owner can capture this week, each with one line on what it shows.
