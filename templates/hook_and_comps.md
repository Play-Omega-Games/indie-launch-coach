# Hook and comparable games

## Hook
Ask the owner for three things, one at a time: what the player does, what makes it feel different, and the mood. Then offer three hooks to choose from:
- **Verb first:** "{verb} {thing} in a {setting} where {twist}."
- **Feeling first:** "A {mood} {genre} about {core idea}."
- **Contrast:** "{familiar thing}, but {surprising thing}."
Rules: one sentence, no hype words ("revolutionary", "unique"), no claims you cannot show in a clip.

## Comparable games
Ask for five games the owner's players already love. For each, note from its public store page on the day you read it: top tags, price, release month, and the kind of thing players praise in reviews. Record the page link and the date read. Never estimate another game's sales.
