# Post drafts

Every post: one idea, one visual, the home base link with that channel's tracking link. No fake urgency. No numbers the owner has not given. Ends with buttons "Approve", "Edit", "Different idea", "Skip". Approved posts are for the owner to post; the coach never posts.

- **X or Bluesky:** a hook line, one clip or GIF, the link. Short.
- **Reddit:** read the subreddit's rules first and follow them. Lead with something useful or interesting; say plainly that you made it.
- **TikTok or Shorts caption:** one line that sets up the clip; the link goes where the platform allows.
- **YouTube description:** two lines, the link, platforms and date window.
- **Discord announcement:** what changed, why it matters, what you want people to try.
- **Steam event or announcement:** a title, a header image, short sections, and what is new.
- **Reveal, demo, festival, launch:** the moment, where to play or wishlist, and when.
