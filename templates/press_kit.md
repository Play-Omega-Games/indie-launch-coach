# Press kit

Use only facts the owner gave. Mark anything missing as "needed from you".

- **Fact sheet:** game name, developer, location, platforms, release date or window, price (if set), languages, home base link, contact email.
- **Short description:** one or two sentences.
- **Long description:** two short paragraphs.
- **Features:** four to six bullet points the build already has.
- **Media:** trailer link, 6 to 10 screenshots, logo (transparent background), key art, capsule.
- **Team:** names and roles the owner chooses to share.
- **Quotes and awards:** only real ones, with where they came from.
- **Keys:** how to ask for a review copy.
Output: a draft page plus a checklist of missing items.
