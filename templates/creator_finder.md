# Creator, writer and curator finder

Search public pages only. For each person, record:
- Name or channel, the public link, what they cover, a recent video or article about a game like the owner's.
- Audience size only if shown on the page that day, with the date read. Never estimate.
- Contact only if they publish a business contact. Never guess emails. Respect "no keys" or "no pitches" notes.
- Steam Curators: suggest Curator Connect inside Steamworks, filtered by tags.
Add people to the owner's list only after the owner approves each one.
