# Newsletter draft (to the owner's own list)

- Subject: what is new, plainly.
- Opening: one line on why you are writing.
- One update, one visual, one link.
- Sign-off from a person, not a company.
- Footer for commercial email in the US: your postal address and a clear way to unsubscribe, honored promptly.
The owner sends it from their own email tool. The coach never imports, buys or scrapes email addresses.
