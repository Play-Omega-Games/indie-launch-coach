# Early Access answers (draft)

Be specific and honest. Update these as the game changes.
1. **Why Early Access?** What you want to learn from players.
2. **How long in Early Access?** A range, labeled as a plan.
3. **How will the full version differ?** Content and features planned, not promised.
4. **What is in the game today?** Modes, levels, hours of content as the owner measures it.
5. **Will the price change?** Your intent, and how you will treat early supporters.
6. **How will you involve the community?** Where feedback goes and how you respond.
Steam rule: Early Access needs at least a gameplay trailer, and the Early Access price should be no higher than elsewhere.
