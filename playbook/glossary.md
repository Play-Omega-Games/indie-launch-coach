# Glossary

- **Home base:** the one link all your posts point to, where interest is counted.
- **Coming Soon page:** a public Steam store page before release. It enables wishlists and your Community Hub.
- **Wishlist:** a player saving your game on Steam. Steam notifies wishlisters at release and for qualifying discounts.
- **Demo notice:** a one-time Steam notification to wishlisters when you release a free demo, sent by you within two weeks of the demo release.
- **Next Fest:** Steam's demo festival, three times a year. One per game.
- **Themed event:** a Steam event around a genre or theme. Steam invites eligible games.
- **Seasonal sale:** Steam's four big sales a year. Open to games released at least 30 days before the start.
- **Launch discount:** an optional Steam discount at release, 7 to 14 days, at most 40%.
- **Curator Connect:** a Steam tool to send review copies to Steam Curators with a personal note.
- **Tracking link (UTM link):** your store link with labels added, so you can see which channel brought visits, wishlists and purchases.
- **Press kit:** one page with your facts, art, trailer and contact, for writers and creators.
- **Run sheet:** your hour-by-hour plan for launch day.
- **Gate:** a platform rule with a date attached. Gates come first in your weekly tasks.
- **Guideline:** Omega Games advice. Useful, not a rule.
