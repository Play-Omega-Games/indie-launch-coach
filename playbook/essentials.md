# The essentials track (under 3 hours a week)

For solo developers with very little time. Everything else is optional.

1. **Hook and home base.** One sentence, one link.
2. **Store page done right.** Readable capsule, at least 5 gameplay screenshots, a clear short description, at least 5 tags (Steam rule), reviewed with the coach before you submit.
3. **The platform deadlines.** Coming Soon review time, the two-week Coming Soon minimum, build review, date lock, and your festival dates if you chose one.
4. **One channel.** One post a week, always with your home base link.
5. **Five personal emails a month** to creators who already play games like yours, starting about two months before launch.
6. **Your numbers, once a week.** Thirty seconds at check-in.
7. **A launch run sheet.** So the day itself is calm.
